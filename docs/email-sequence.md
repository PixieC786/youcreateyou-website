## Email 17 — Day 50 — A full practice, no strings

**Subject:** Here's a whole practice, free
**Preheader:** Inner Cinema — the real version, not a teaser this time.

---

A few weeks ago we mentioned Inner Cinema — the difference between watching your life from the outside, and stepping inside the version you're actually building.

Here's the full practice. Takes about five minutes. No app, no book required.

Close your eyes. Pick one moment from a day you want — not a vague "someday," a specific moment. Morning coffee. A conversation. Walking through a door.

Now stop watching it. Step inside it. Feel your own feet in that moment, not someone else's feet you're observing. What are you wearing? What do you smell? Who's talking, and what does their voice sound like from inside the scene, not from a seat in the audience?

Stay there for a full minute before you open your eyes.

That's it. That's the whole practice. Do it daily and something starts to shift in how your day actually goes — not because of magic, because you've started rehearsing the version of you that lives there instead of the version watching from the sidelines.

— You Create You
